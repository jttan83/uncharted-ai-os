# Uncharted Lab Brand QA

## Output
Return: **Verdict** (Pass / Pass with fixes / Not ready), **Critical fixes**, **Recommended refinements**, **Evidence / claim risks**, and **Final approval checklist**. Never self-approve on Jentz's behalf.

## Audit
- Source: approved Brand System and, for presentations, Master Presentation System v2; canonical master preserved; approved layouts reused.
- Brand: supports MAKE CHANGE WORKABLE; clear, human, practical, evidence-aware; starts with the work/problem.
- Type: Anton for short display text; Inter for body/supporting information; readable sizes; selective uppercase.
- Colour: #000000, #CDFC54, #F7F3DB, #FFFFFF; lime directs attention rather than dominating; accessible contrast.
- Graphics: bracket purposeful; lines/frames/blocks organise; no gradients, 3D, blobs, random shapes, or filler; empty space is intentional.
- Footer/logo: standard content slides use subtle unchartedlab.co in approved bottom-right position; footer never lime; cover/formal closing use approved logo; logo not stretched, recoloured, recreated, or given effects.
- Photography: adds evidence/context/meaning; documentary/specific over staged stock; no AI imagery presented as evidence; prefer evidence or empty space when no meaningful image exists.
- Icons: functional, consistent, one family; no decorative icon use.
- Charts: simplest appropriate chart; finding obvious; labels/units/timeframe/source where relevant; honest axes/scales; no 3D or misleading decoration.
- Voice: clear, curious, practical; thinking partner not guru; simple English, active voice; no hype, clichés, jargon, or generic AI copy.
- Evidence: no invented clients, credentials, testimonials, statistics, or outcomes; evidence distinguished from inference; unsupported claims flagged; missing proof uses explicit placeholders; case studies do not imply unmeasured outcomes.
- Logic: one clear idea per slide/page where practical; insight-led headlines; logical movement toward decision/action; only necessary slides/pages.

## Severity
- Critical: fabricated/unsupported claim, misleading chart, serious confidentiality issue, unreadable content, wrong identity.
- Major: inconsistent typography/palette, missing footer, generic imagery, unclear hierarchy, off-brand voice, new visual language.
- Minor: spacing, alignment, small copy refinements, optional polish.

## Final approval checklist
Brand system consistent; evidence verified; client-sensitive information cleared; footer/logo correct; readability checked; links/contact details checked; Jentz final approval obtained.
