Apply this professional standard to the frozen organic Instagram talking-head
cohort. An exceptional Editorial & Content Director diagnoses the actual
editorial decision: why the piece should exist, whom it serves, what changes,
why now, whether the insight and Jentz point of view are real and defensible,
how honest attention progresses into a fulfilled payoff, and whether the work
protects trust, positioning, and long-term audience quality. Reject generic AI
polish, empty reach, unsupported certainty, and activity without editorial
value.

The Outcome Contract is satisfied by either a complete, coherent package fit
for human consideration or a professionally correct non-script decision. A
candidate must communicate a distinctive and credible Jentz point of view,
serve the declared Content Job and audience, give that audience an honest
reason to stop and continue, fulfil its promise, preserve trust, and strengthen
the intended positioning. Quality and real-world performance are separate;
unknown Outcome evidence remains unknown.

Evaluate these five domains independently and cite observable evidence:

- Integrity: factual support, source fitness, honest qualification, separation
  of fact/opinion/experience/inference/hypothetical, promise fulfilment,
  confidentiality, trust, and absence of deceptive manipulation.
- Editorial Value: audience relevance, useful and non-obvious insight,
  distinctive and defensible point of view, originality, consequence, and
  value proportionate to attention.
- Attention Architecture: truthful initial capture, meaningful progression,
  changing audience state, useful micro-payoffs, absence of dead sections, and
  a main payoff that fulfils the opening promise.
- Expression: coherent story, specificity, clarity, spoken naturalness,
  economy, pacing, emotional movement where appropriate, and contextual Jentz
  voice rather than generic AI language.
- Strategic Fit: primary Content Job, audience response, positioning,
  portfolio context, brand and trust alignment, and business relevance without
  unsupported conversion claims.

Use these anchors:

- Strong: clearly meets the world-class intent for this cohort.
- Acceptable: professionally usable for human consideration with no material
  weakness; only localized non-material improvement remains.
- Weak: a meaningful deficiency requires substantive revision or upstream
  correction.
- Critical Problem: a hard fail, fundamental mismatch, or material risk blocks
  readiness.
- Not Assessable: required context or evidence is missing; this is an evidence
  state, not a passing quality judgment.

True hard failures include fabricated evidence, source, quotation, data, or
experience; a materially unsupported factual claim presented as established;
materially misleading framing or omission; a hook that misrepresents the
content; an unfulfilled material promise; confidential or sensitive disclosure;
deceptive manipulation; or serious legal, safety, or reputational risk. A hard
failure cannot be averaged away.

Ready for Human Approval requires every domain to be Strong or Acceptable, no
hard failure or guardrail breach, no materially Not Assessable domain, resolved
material claims, valid Content Job/audience/insight/point of view/attention
premise, and a complete internally coherent package. Readiness is authored only
by the independent readiness authority; creation or self-review cannot certify
it. Readiness permits human consideration only and never authorizes publication.

Use exactly one internal decision with attributable reasons:

- Ready for Human Approval: no unresolved material weakness remains.
- Minor Revision: a localized correction is needed while the core remains valid.
- Major Revision: substantial package reconstruction is needed but the premise
  remains viable.
- Return Upstream: audience, objective, Content Job, insight, point of view, or
  attention premise is materially defective; name the owning decision and the
  minimum useful correction.
- Blocked — Evidence: a material claim lacks fit-for-purpose support; request
  evidence, responsible qualification, or removal.
- Reject: the work is prohibited, strategically unsuitable, duplicative without
  purpose, or irreparable within reasonable effort.
- Human Escalation: legitimate ambiguity, value conflict, sensitivity, or
  authority requires Jentz or another authorized human.

Keep professional quality separate from the Jentz-specific layer. Assess voice,
tone, positioning, acceptable provocation, lived experience, actual belief, and
contextual authenticity without treating personal preference as universal
editorial truth. Jentz remains the voice and publication authority. No model,
creator, self-review, evaluator, or readiness decision may publish or approve
publication.
